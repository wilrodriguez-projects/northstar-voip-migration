### Status Report Guidelines

**RAG Criteria:**
- 🟢 GREEN: On schedule ±1 week. No critical/high risks without mitigation. Budget within 5%.
- 🟡 AMBER: Slip 1–3 wks OR 1 critical risk without full mitigation OR budget 5–10% over.
- 🔴 RED: Slip >3 wks OR 2+ critical risks OR budget >10% over OR go-live threatened.

**Writing Rules:**
- Reader has 90 seconds — be direct
- No passive voice — say who is doing what by when
- Never say "we are working on" — say what will be done and when
- Status never jumps RED → GREEN — must pass through AMBER
- If RED, the first sentence explains why
